      HOW IT WORKS

      A graph, not a perfect ranking

      Each arrow goes from a winner to a person they beat. The layout tries to place stronger upstream records above weaker downstream records.

      • Global records come from global-graph.json and are the same for everyone.
      • Local records live only in this browser until exported.
      • Cycles are allowed and shown with dashed highlighted arrows.
      • Reach counts distinct people downstream through direct or indirect wins.

      Delete local additions?

      This removes every match and person stored by this site in this browser. Global data will not change.
